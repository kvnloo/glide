Guard your attention
and the agent's context.

Glide holds an agent's non-critical asks while you work and hands them back as one digest. It also quotes your own past corrections into the next turn, so you stop repeating yourself. It all renders as native cards in Tern.

Play the demo
hermes plugins install kvnloo/glide

Try it here

This is a scripted browser demo that runs on the synthetic sessions from glide/demo/seed.py. It makes no model calls and nothing leaves this page. In Tern, the same steps draw as native cards, and /theme uses Tern's real colours; the ones here are approximations.

hermes glide Dash

Start with /glide

Type a command below or tap a chip. Each write waits for your approval button, the same as in Tern.

01

Your attention

/glide focus shows your phase, your next deep-work window and your bedtime. A block holds the agent's non-critical asks until it ends.

  1. Focus card. Your phase, next window, bedtime and how many asks are held. Flow likelihood shows only with a live Flowkit estimate.
  2. Start block. Approve a 50-minute block. Replies stay short and asks routed through glide.route wait. Critical ones still pass.
  3. End block. One digest card lists everything that waited.
  4. Wind-down. /glide focus wind-down saves your open asks. The next morning's first turn quotes them once.
  5. Your hours. /glide focus hours learns your best two-hour windows from your own local session timestamps.

02

The agent's context

Opt-in corrections memory quotes up to three matching past corrections into a turn. Offload sends small tasks to a local model.

  1. Detect. Glide inspects CPU, RAM, GPU and local runtimes. Detection installs nothing.
  2. Build memory. Approve a read of up to 100 recent local sessions. Glide keeps the lines that begin with Correction:, Actually, or No,.
  3. Turn on. Approve the recall hook. Current instructions take precedence over quoted history.

Extraction and BM25 ranking make no model calls, and the source database is opened read-only. Once recall is on, quoted excerpts enter the model's context and can reach your configured provider. Storage and privacy contract

03

More in the chat

Each command shows local state as a native card. Every card uses one design language: omp's card grammar and Dash's palette.

/theme
Tern, Nous, the Hermes skin, your wallpaper (live) or Dash's black and gold.
/glide glass
A liquid-glass demo card, the only surface allowed to glow.
/glide taste
The newest Taste Atlas board beside the chat.
/glide lanes
Live-voice helper lanes, their model and billing.
/intel
Local intelligence: hill climbs, skills, memory, failures, traces.
/hosts
Fleet hosts, agents, gauges and k3s pods.
/factory
The content factory graph, runs and renders.
/council
A Socratic council popup with one verdict per seat.

05

Install

You need Hermes Agent 0.23.0 or later, Tern and Node.js.

hermes plugins install kvnloo/glide

Review Hermes' scanner findings before you approve. It may report CAUTION, as it does for the base Tern plugin. Then accept the plugin-enable prompt.

hermes tern

Run it in a Tern pane, outside tmux, screen or zellij. Then start with /glide. An ordinary terminal cannot render the Surface Protocol.

bash scripts/e2e-fresh-install.sh

From a checkout, this installs into a throwaway HERMES_HOME, validates the plugin and exercises memory, focus and Taste with synthetic data. It never touches your profile or calls a model.
