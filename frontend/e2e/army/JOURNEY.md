# Glide qualification journey

`goals.tsv` is the executable lane list. Kit lanes cover hosts, portfolio, factory, themes, and phone touch. Tern lanes cover those cards plus `/glide` approvals, focus, taste, recall, voice states, transcript actions, and keyboard/overlay/resize behavior.

Every goal requires interaction and scrolling across sections. A single screenshot is not completion. Empty synthetic history, unavailable integrations, provider failures, and unavailable browser automation are recorded explicitly. Do not substitute synthetic claims for microphone, native shortcut, or physical mobile acceptance.

The smoke runs only two steps per target kind. Full journey and premium sanity review are separate later runs.
